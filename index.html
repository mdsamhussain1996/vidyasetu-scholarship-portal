Why I'm making this

Every academic year, I meet students with real curiosity and sharp minds who decide not to continue their studies simply because they do not know that someone is ready to pay for their tuition and living costs. Most government and charitable schemes in India never appear on billboards or television; they are quietly announced in an official circular on a departmental portal and left there for students to find.

Many commercial portals turn this into a business by hiding links behind paywalls, sending promotional phone calls, or steering students toward private colleges. VidyaSetu is my small attempt to fix this. It has no advertisements, no tracking, and no fees. Every entry links directly to the official government, university or trust website.

If you are a student, remember this simple rule: applying for genuine scholarships is always free. Gather your paperwork early, keep your bank account linked to your Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), and apply directly on the official portals.

Md Samshad Hussain Ansari
